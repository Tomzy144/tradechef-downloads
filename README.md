## 🏦 Step 1: Open a Trading Account

Before using TradeChef, you need a trading account with one of our supported brokers, preferable HFM.

TradeChef currently supports the following brokers:

### 1. Headway

Create your Headway trading account using our partnership registration link:

[**Register with Headway**](https://headway.partners/user/signup?hwp=6fe98a)

Follow the broker's registration process and complete any required identity verification.

Once your account is approved:

1. Sign in to your Headway client area.
2. Create a trading account.
3. Select the supported trading platform (MT5, where available).
4. Choose your preferred account type and currency.
5. Record your trading account number, trading password and server name.

### 2. HFM (HFMarkets)

Create your HFM account using our registration link:

[**Register with HFM**](https://www.hfm.com/sv/en/?refid=30575707)

After registration:

1. Complete the required account verification.
2. Sign in to your HFM client area.
3. Create a trading account.
4. Select MT5 if available for your account.
5. Record your trading account number, trading password and server name.

You can also download the official HFM mobile application:

[**Download HFM App**](https://www.hfm.com/sv/en/hfapp?refid=30575707)

## 📱 Step 2: Set Up TradeChef

After creating your broker account:

1. Open TradeChef on your Android device.
2. Sign in or create your TradeChef account.
3. Navigate to the trading account connection section.
4. Select your broker.
5. Select the appropriate trading platform.
6. Enter your trading account details.

You will generally need:

| Field            | Description                                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Broker           | Headway or HFM                                   |
| Account Number   | Your broker-issued trading account number        |
| Trading Password | Your MT5 trading password                        |
| Server           | The exact trading server assigned by your broker |
|                  |                                                  |

7. Double-check your details.
8. Submit the connection request.
9. Wait for TradeChef to establish the connection.

> [!WARNING]
> Enter your trading credentials only inside the official TradeChef application. Never share your trading password with another person.

## 🤖 Step 3: Get Your Trading Bot Running

Once your trading account is connected:

1. Open the bot dashboard.
2. Confirm that your trading account is connected.
3. Review the available bot settings.
4. Configure your preferred trading parameters.
5. Review your risk settings carefully.
6. Start the bot using the activation control on the dashboard.
7. Monitor the bot's connection and trading status.

**Important:** Make sure you understand the trading strategy and risk settings before activating automated trading.

## 🧪 Step 4: Start With a Demo Account

If you are new to automated trading, we strongly recommend starting with a broker demo account.

A demo account allows you to:

- Understand how TradeChef operates.
- Test the connection between TradeChef and your broker.
- Observe the bot's trading behaviour.
- Familiarise yourself with the available settings.

Only consider using a live trading account after understanding the associated risks.
